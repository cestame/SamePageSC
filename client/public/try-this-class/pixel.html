How the real version works: a 30-second scan of the empty room becomes a 3D model (Gaussian splatting), rendered at low resolution in this pixel style. Classmates are anonymous figures driven by aggregate data: attendance, hand-raise rates, and bursts of discussion detected from audio. No real student is ever shown. The professor's moves follow their teaching-move timeline from recorded sessions. The pixel style is deliberate: it shows how the room works without imitating any real person.
