Before class

Audio never leaves this laptop. Whisper turns speech into text here, a voice model tells speakers apart, and each audio clip is deleted as soon as it is processed. Other students' words are never shown or saved, only that they spoke and for how long.

Teach it your voice

Read this out loud: "I'm checking in for class today, and I want Same Page to recognize my voice when I ask a question."

Without a professor sample, the voice that talks the most is treated as the professor.
